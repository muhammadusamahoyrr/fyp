# Sources for the legal playbook drafts

**Status: DRAFTS FOR LAWYER REVIEW. None of these packs is approved.** A legal
pack runs only after a reviewer holding its `required_qualification` approves
its exact text hash AND the hash of the official provisions it cites
(DOC_ANALYZER_PLAN.md §4.5a). Until then the analyzer reports each one as
"not approved by a qualified lawyer yet, so it was not run".

## Where the cited text lives

The text of every cited provision is in
[`../authority_library.yaml`](../authority_library.yaml), cut from the
**official** publications and pinned by the SHA-256 of each file. It is
generated by `scripts/build_analyzer_authorities.py`, which refuses to build
if an official file has changed. A pack that cites a provision not in the
library does not load. A reviewer reads the cited text through
`GET /documents/analyses/playbooks/{id}` (`cited_provisions`).

## What verification against the official text found

* **Punjab Rented Premises Act.** The first draft quoted a secondary
  publisher (nasirlawsite.com) because the official site was unreachable.
  Compared with the official Punjab Code PDF, ss.5 and 7 matched word for
  word; s.6 differed in two places, both errors in the secondary copy:
  s.6(1)(d) reads "mode of payment **of** rent" (not "or rent"), and
  s.6(1)(f) ends "let out; and". The official PDF carries no amendment
  notes, and a Punjab Code search for amending Acts returned none --
  **currency is still for the reviewer to confirm**.
* **Transfer of Property Act s.107 territorial reach.** The official s.1
  footnotes record that ss.54, 107 and 123 were extended from 6 May 1935 to
  all Municipalities in the Punjab and notified areas under s.241 of the
  Punjab Municipal Act 1911. The tenancy pack now cites s.1 so the reviewer
  sees it; whether s.107 reaches a given property is for the reviewer.
* **The chat corpus is not used for citations.** In the Chroma corpus the
  only chunk labelled TPA "107" is a footnote; the section text sits under
  another label. Registration Act 1908 and Contract Act 1872 are not in the
  corpus at all. See "Not done" below.

## Text conventions (from the publishers' PDF text layer)

* Footnote numbers are inline: "2107. Leases how made" is footnote 2
  followed by "107."; "4[Provincial Government]" marks an amended phrase.
* Footnotes printed at the foot of a page appear where the page breaks,
  e.g. inside s.28 of the Contract Act.
* U+037E (Greek question mark) is normalised to ";" and U+2011 (non-breaking
  hyphen) to "-"; "Page N of M" footers are dropped. Nothing else is changed.

## Not cited, deliberately

* **Employment statutes** (standing orders, provincial labour codes). No
  official text has been verified, so no employment-specific provision is
  cited. The employment pack's statutory-protection item is an uncited
  **reminder** for the qualified reviewer to replace with the correct
  provisions -- which must then be added to the library before the pack loads.
* **Stamp duty, Specific Relief Act.** Not used by any check yet.
