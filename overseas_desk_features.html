Overseas Desk

01

My Powers of Attorney

A full POA lifecycle manager built around one fact: a court reads the written authority, not what was intended — so choosing the wrong type of POA is the single biggest cause of misuse and fraud.

◆Plain-English POA advisorType an intent ("let my brother sell my flat"); an LLM maps it to a POA type + powers, then a pure rule engine enforces the non-negotiable safety rules — disposing of property forces a Special (registered) POA, a rule that lives in code, not the prompt.
◆Fraud-risk scoreDeterministic scoring flags fraud-prone shapes: a General POA carrying disposal powers, no expiry, vague scope, an over-broad grant.
◆POA generationSpecial / General POA as a PDF with a non-removable DRAFT-FOR-LEGAL-REVIEW banner.
◆Disposal-power guardSell / transfer / gift / mortgage powers are barred from a General POA at the data layer.
◆Registry & revocationTracks every POA, warns before expiry, and generates a Deed of Revocation in one click.
◆Attestation lifecycleExecution workflow: drafted → notarised → mission attested → MOFA attested → registered.
◆Tamper-evidenceEach PDF's SHA-256 hash is stored so later tampering can be detected.
◆Point-of-use verify link + QRA public, no-login link (the token is the capability). A buyer's lawyer or sub-registrar scans the QR and sees the POA's live status, exact authorised scope, and tamper-hash — stopping a revoked or forged POA from being honoured.
◆Attested-document verifyUpload the returned attested POA; the system reports the attestation markers found and whether they match the POA on record — "did they attest the right thing?"
◆OPPPA statusTrack whether the property is registered with the Overseas Pakistanis' Property Protection Authority.
02

Attestation Navigator

Objection-aware routing between the apostille and the legacy consular chain. Pakistan joined the Hague Apostille Convention (in force 9 March 2023, Apostille Act 2024): a single apostille can replace the old Notary → Mission → MOFA legalisation chain.

But it is not "apostille everywhere"
  • Bilateral objections can't be inferred from membership. Several states objected under Art. 12, so the Convention doesn't operate between them and Pakistan — the legacy chain is still correct.
  • India is a separate case (political non-recognition, not an Art. 12 objection) with the same practical result.
  • Unknown countries fail safe to the longer legacy chain, never the shorter apostille path.

Pick the country where the POA is executed; get the correct route.

03

Special Courts

A jurisdiction engine for the remedy most overseas Pakistanis don't know exists. The 2024 Act created special courts — with e-filing, video-link hearings supervised by Pakistan missions, and statutory deadlines — and provinces are enacting their own versions on a rolling basis.

◆Per-province resolutionFederal/ICT is operational; some provinces are enacted-but-pending; others have nothing yet.
◆Real timelinesDisposal window measured from the grant of leave to defend (not from filing), plus the appeal window where known.
◆Fail-safe routingA court not confirmed operational is never told to "e-file here today" — it routes to the federal framework and a lawyer, flagged for verification.
◆Dated & sourcedEvery figure carries an effective_as_of date, a confidence tier (established / single-source / unconfirmed), and a source citation.
04

Property Dispute

The fast-track enforcement flow: from "my property was grabbed" to either a drafted petition or a lawyer's desk — never a guess.

Guided 4-step pipeline
1

Eligibility

A deterministic (no-LLM) check of overseas-Pakistani status: a valid ID (passport / CNIC / NICOP / POC / OPF) plus 182+ days abroad in a tax year.

2

Classify the grievance

A grounded LLM sorts the complaint into one of six categories with a confidence gate. This is the one place AI heads toward a real filing, so an unconfirmed or ambiguous result never proceeds — it holds for a lawyer. The hold is enforced in pure code, not the prompt.

3

Guided intake

Fixed fields only — property, province, opposing party, timeline, documents held, relief sought. No open-ended conversational intake.

4

Outcome

The pipeline ends in exactly one of two states:

✓ Ready to draft

Generates a Special-Court petition PDF. Assembly is deterministic (heading, parties, jurisdiction, relief, leave-to-defend timing, the confirmed 15-day appeal window); the LLM writes only the facts & cause of action, grounded strictly in the intake — "[to be provided]" rather than an invention. Missing a field refuses the draft. Nothing is auto-filed.

⏸ Held for triage

The client gets a plain-language notification explaining why it's held, with a route into the verified-lawyer directory.

⇢ Case-brief handoff to a lawyer read / handoff only
  • Send to lawyer — from either state, packages the whole case (eligibility, classification + confidence, entered facts, jurisdiction, and the draft petition if present) to one verified lawyer. Idempotent — can't be re-sent.
  • Lawyer inbox — a page listing every case brief sent to that lawyer, with state and "petition ready" badges.
  • Case brief view — the lawyer opens the whole case in one place and downloads the draft petition where present.
  • Deep-link notification — the "new case brief" alert opens the inbox directly.
No fee negotiation · no engagement letter · no payment — that is the separate hiring flow.

The through-line

Design principles in every feature

🛡️

Fail-safe / fail-closed

Uncertainty routes to a human, never a confident guess.

⚙️

AI suggests, code enforces

Every safety-critical rule — disposal → Special POA, the triage hold, grounding — lives in deterministic code, not a prompt.

🗓️

Dated & sourced

Fast-moving legal facts carry an effective date, a confidence tier, a citation, and a standing "verify with a lawyer" duty.

📎

Grounded generation

Drafts use only the facts provided; a gap becomes "[to be provided]", never an invention.

📄

Nothing filed or paid automatically

Every generated document is a DRAFT for a qualified lawyer to review, complete and file.
